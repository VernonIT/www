## About VernonIT

VernonIT is a full-lifecycle product engineering practice run by David Vernon. We partner with startups and growth-stage companies to design, build, and ship software — from architecture through to production.

## What we do

We specialise in web applications, backend infrastructure, and platform engineering. Whether you're building from scratch or scaling an existing system, we bring the technical depth to get it done without the overhead of a large agency.

## Background

David Vernon has spent the better part of two decades building and shipping software across fintech, healthcare, logistics, and SaaS. Past work spans distributed systems, API platform design, cloud infrastructure on Azure and AWS, and leading engineering teams through rapid growth.
