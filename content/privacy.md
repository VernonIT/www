### Information We Collect

**Analytics.** This site uses [GoatCounter](https://www.goatcounter.com/), a privacy-friendly analytics tool. GoatCounter does not use cookies, does not track users across sites, and does not collect personal data. It records only aggregate page view counts.

**Contact form.** When you submit the contact form, your name, email address, and message are sent to us via [Formspree](https://formspree.io/). We use this information solely to respond to your inquiry and do not share it with third parties.
